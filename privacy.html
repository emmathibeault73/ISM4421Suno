Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

SongForge is a web app for creating songs with AI. This policy explains what information it handles and why.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and, if you sign up with a password, your securely hashed password.
  • Google sign-in: if you continue with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. We don't request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or other Google data.
  • Profile details you choose to add: username, display name, bio, profile picture, favorite genres, and default model.

Information stored only in your browser

Your Suno API key, song library, and some preferences are saved in your browser's local storage. They aren't saved to our database. Clearing your browser data removes them.

How we use information

We use it to sign you in, show and save your profile, and run the app. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase provides sign-in, the database, and profile picture storage. Uploaded profile pictures are available at a public web address.
  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • Suno API (sunoapi.org): when you generate music, your song description, settings, and API key go directly from your browser to this service.
  • Google provides Google sign-in and the fonts on this site, which means your browser connects to Google when loading them.

Each service handles information under its own privacy terms.

Google user data

SongForge's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to sign you in and set up your profile.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep account and profile information while your account exists. To delete your account and profile, email emmabelz18@gmail.com from your account's email address. You can also revoke SongForge's access in your Google Account settings.

Children

SongForge isn't intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the update here and change the date above.

Contact

Questions? Email emmabelz18@gmail.com.
